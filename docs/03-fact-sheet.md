# Fact sheet: every claim in the product section, with its source

Facts as of **October 2026**. Each line lists where the claim appears, the source it rests on, and what compliance still needs to do before print.

Most sources below are reputable secondary coverage (financial press, law-firm notes, insurer and AMC explainers). Before sign-off, compliance should check each **Verify** item against the primary text on the regulator's website (SEBI, IRDAI, IFSCA, RBI, CBDT).

Status key:

- **Confirmed**: several independent sources agree, and the fact is long-standing.
- **Verify**: the fact is recent, comes from a single source, or the exact figure matters. Check the primary document.
- **Wording**: the text is a regulatory warning or disclaimer, so compliance must confirm the exact wording.

## Facts deliberately left out

- **Returns, past performance and premium quotes.** These date quickly and trigger extra advertising rules. Every number on the pages is a rule, a limit or labelled arithmetic.
- **Scheme, fund, insurer or issuer names.** The section explains categories, not products, so it reads as investor education rather than an advertisement for a specific scheme.
- **Industry size figures** (for example, SIF assets of ₹38,224 crore across 17 AMCs at September 2026, per [5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/blog/sif-industry-aum-nears-40000-crore)). They would be stale within weeks of printing.
