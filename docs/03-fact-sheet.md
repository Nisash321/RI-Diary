# Fact sheet: every claim in the product section, with its source

Facts as of **October 2026**. Each line lists where the claim appears, the source it rests on, and what compliance still needs to do before print.

Most sources below are reputable secondary coverage (financial press, law-firm notes, insurer and AMC explainers). Before sign-off, compliance should check each **Verify** item against the primary text on the regulator's website (SEBI, IRDAI, IFSCA, RBI, CBDT) or the industry body's data (AMFI).

Status key:

- **Confirmed**: several independent sources agree, and the fact is long-standing.
- **Verify**: the fact is recent, comes from a single source, or the exact figure matters. Check the primary document.
- **Wording**: the text is a regulatory warning or disclaimer, so compliance must confirm the exact wording.
- **Refresh**: industry data that will move before print. Update it, and its "as of" label, at the last proof.

House rule: the pages give no returns and no projections (see section 5 of [02-framework.md](02-framework.md)). Figures researched but held back under that rule are listed at the end.
