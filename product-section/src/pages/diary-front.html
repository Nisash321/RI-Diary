Nil band: ₹3 lakh at age 60 to 79; ₹5 lakh at 80 and above. Standard deduction: ₹50,000. Deductions such as Sections 123 and 126 apply (page iii).

No tax on income up to ₹12 lakh

New regime: a rebate makes the tax nil if total income is up to ₹12 lakh (₹12.75 lakh for salaried people). Old regime: up to ₹5 lakh. Income taxed at special rates, such as capital gains, is not covered.

Cash, gifts and property

  • No cash receipts of ₹2 lakh or more from one person in a day, or for one event.
  • Do not take or give a loan or deposit of ₹20,000 or more in cash.
  • Gifts above ₹50,000 a year from non-relatives are taxed as your income.
  • Property sales are taxed on the stamp-duty value if it is higher than the price.
