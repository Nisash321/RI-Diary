Since 1985

Four decades of trust

In 1985, L. Ashok and N. Alagar founded Ram Investments in Dindigul with one aim: to help people invest their hard-earned money with confidence and clarity. Forty years on, our clients’ success still comes first.

  1. 1985

    Founded in Dindigul

    We began with postal deposits, helping families with their first savings.

  2. Growing

    Mutual funds and more

    As India’s markets opened up, we added mutual funds, insurance and a wider range of investments.

  3. Recognised

    Among the best in the state

    Consistently among the top 10 performers at the state level, with awards from government agencies, including honours from the Hon’ble Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu.

  4. Today

    The next generation

    With Nishanth Ashok, we now offer everything from mutual funds and insurance to SIFs, PMS, AIFs and international investing through GIFT City.

Our promise to you

We listen first. We learn your goals and how much risk you are comfortable with, and suggest only what fits them. When markets or rules change, we tell you what it means for you.
