Private markets

Where the early growth happens

Indian companies now raise money privately for a decade or more before they list. By the time most investors can buy at the IPO, much of that early growth has already happened. AIFs are how individual investors can take part earlier.

What this means for you

An IPO is often when early investors sell part of their stake to the public. To own a share of a company in its private years, you invest before it lists, through an AIF. That means a long wait, and accepting that some companies never list, or list below their private value.

Choose the manager with careResults vary widely

Outcomes differ far more from one private-market fund to the next than between funds that invest in listed shares. Ask how long the team has worked together, what happened in its earlier funds, and how it handled difficult years.
