SIF in depth

Using SIFs in your portfolio

Three ways to use a SIFMixes shown are examples

As your whole portfolio

No mutual funds yet? An asset allocator strategy can hold shares, bonds, gold, silver and REITs in one fund, and shift between them.

As the conservative part

Hybrid and debt strategies can keep most money in arbitrage and bonds, with a small share in stocks, aiming for smaller swings than equity funds.

As the equity part

Equity long-short strategies can sit beside your equity funds, and can hedge or cut exposure when markets turn.

Tax in practice: when the tax is paidSame trades, three wrappers

Gains made inside a SIF are not taxed along the way, so that money stays invested until you redeem, and you choose when. Rates are in the tax check in entry 04.
