How we help you

Many of these products can be bought online. Families still come to us, because the hard part is not buying: it is choosing well and staying on course for years. This is what we do for you.

  1. We stay with you when markets fallWe talk it through with you, so a bad month does not undo a long-term plan, and your SIPs keep going.
  2. We match each product to a goalWe start from your goals, timelines and comfort with risk, and explain what you are buying before you sign.
  3. We look at everything togetherFunds, SIFs, NCDs, PMS, AIFs and insurance, reviewed side by side with you, at least once a year.
  4. We handle the paperworkKYC, nominees, bank and address changes, SIPs, switches and redemptions, so nothing stalls.
  5. We help at tax timeCapital gains statements and the papers your tax consultant asks for, for every product you hold with us.
  6. We stand by your familyIf something happens to you, we help your family with insurance claims and with moving investments into their names.
  7. We keep you up to dateWhen rules or taxes change, we tell you what it means for you, such as the new PMS rules from April {{year}}.
  8. We are a phone call awayThe same local team in Dindigul since 1985, for you and for the next generation of your family.

How we are paid

We receive commission from the providers of the products we distribute. It pays for this service, for as long as you invest with us.
