Four jobs your money can do

Read from left to right. Each job works best once the one before it is in place: protect your family before you invest, and build a core portfolio before you add PMS or AIF.

Ready for the next step?Tick what is already in place

  • Term cover that matches your family’s needs, often 10 to 15 times yearly income
  • Health cover for every member of your family, not only through an employer
  • An emergency fund of about six months’ expenses, easy to reach
  • A core portfolio in mutual funds or SIFs, linked to your goals
