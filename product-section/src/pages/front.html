Four jobs your money can do

Read from left to right. Each job works best once the one before it is in place: protection before investing, and a core portfolio before specialist strategies.

Before you move rightTick what is already in place

  • Term cover that matches your family’s needs, often 10 to 15 times yearly income
  • Health cover for every member of your family, not only through an employer
  • An emergency fund of about six months’ expenses, easy to reach
  • A core portfolio, usually in mutual funds, linked to your goals

PRIM is the mutual-fund-only PMS route that starts on 1 April 2027. Insurance is priced by premium and GIFT City minimums are in US dollars, so neither is on the scale above.
